Questions you might have

Fair questions. Plain answers.

What is the free preview?

It's a homepage for your practice, made from what is already public about it. You get to see what your own website would look like before any cost is discussed.

Do I have to write the text?

No. We write the copy from what you already publish, so it takes very little of your time.

When do I pay?

Only after you've seen the preview, liked it and approved in writing. It's one payment: $499 for a one-page website or $1,499 for a full institutional website. There is no monthly fee.

Can I say no after seeing the preview?

Yes. Nothing is published or charged without your written approval.

What do you use to make the preview?

Only what is already public about your practice.

What about timing, domain and hosting?

We agree on those details with you in writing, before anything is charged.

Who are you?

Made by Felipe is a small remote studio based in São Paulo, Brazil. Everything is done online, in English.
